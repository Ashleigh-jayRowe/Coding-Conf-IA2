Seven cures for a lean purse

The first cure for a lean purse

The first cure says that one should save One-tenth or all earnings rather than spend it all prematurely. Saving a little money goes a long way. Saving money and lead to help even the generations after you.

The Second cure for a lean purse

The second cure says that you should budget your expenses and have them spereate form your necessities and your disposable funds. You can buy what you want without spending more that 9/10th of your funds. Arkad made it clear not to confuse necessities with desires sometimes it hard to seperate them and it did cause a deal of confusion among the me but it is one of the most important step.

The third cure for a lean purse

The third cure is pretty much saying to let your money work for you invest your money into thing yk will allow it to multiply. Allowing your money to work for you will help to insure a health stream of wealth will follow you consistently so your purse will never run dry.

The Fourth Cure for a Lean Purse

The fourth cure is saying to network basically talk to other successful people, learn about the moves they made to help make their money multiply. Use their experience to basically protect your money from bad/unsafe investments. Arkad also explained that misfortune loves a shiny mark, so we should know how to protect our gold the Gods entrust us with more.

The fifth Cure for a Lean Purse

This part basically spoke about owning your own house as it was common among the men there to rent their houses. So he basically was explaining that taking out a loan and buying a house with it is better than renting a house your entire life. With renting you basically ending keeping yourself in debt because your always paying to the landlord with clearing any debt. When you take the loan to buy the house every payment on that loan is slowly clearing the debt and the house will be able to be passed down through generation.

The Sixth Cure for a lean Purse

The sixth cure basically he talks about the protection of your assest for your future. He speaks on how the time and place of death is unknow so you should be prepared for the worst. If something were to happen to you, would your familiy still be able to access your funds to help them? Thats what is addressed in this chapter. Ensuring proper preperations are set in place so your money and investments not only benifit you but your familiy and future generations to come.

The Seventh Cure for a lean Purse

The final lesson taught is to cultivate your own powers, to study and become wiser, to become more skillful and to respect yourself. Once you apply all these cures you are to slowly develop your own skills and become even more successful so that you will be able to pass it down to yoour future generations and all other things.

Reflection

The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse really opened my eyes to how recklessly I've been spending my money. For each cure listed within the chapter, I found myself relating in some way, and I now see why I'm always wondering where my money has gone when it has been me spending it all the time. A part that really stood out to me was the part that said, "to put each coin to laboring that it may reproduce its kind even as the flocks of the field and help bring to thee income, a stream of wealth that shall flow constantly into thy purse." To summarize, it is basically saying to let your money work for you. I've always saved some of my money, but the idea of letting that money multiply itself through investing and other methods really opened my eyes to the many ways I can retain and grow my money. Another part that stood out to me was "Own thy own home." It was basically saying that renting a house all your life keeps you in debt, and it would be better to take out a loan and buy a house so that each payment brings you one step closer to owning it. Although I've never owned a house, I have seen both of my parents go from renting to becoming homeowners. I never really understood why we left the house we rented because it was really nice, but this book has enlightened me and made me aspire to become a homeowner as well. I feel like after reading this chapter, I am better able to understand how to properly use my money to its full potential. The biggest lesson I took away from the Seven Cures for a Lean Purse is that I need to be more mindful of my spending habits and think about how the money I have can benefit me in the future. The 5 laws of Gold really just expand on how to spend let money work for you and not loose it on foolish purchase or because of per pressure it really shows the results or being responsible vs being irresponsible with your money.
