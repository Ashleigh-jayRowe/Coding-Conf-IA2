My Hero or Role Model

Kyojuro Rengoku

My hero is Kyojuro Rengoku from Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. Rengoku is a Flame Hashira who is known for his strong sense of responsibility, courage, determination, and willingness to protect others. He continues to encourage the people around him even when facing difficult situations.

I admire Rengoku because of his determination and the way he stays true to his values. He does not allow difficult situations to make him give up, and he always tries to remain positive and encourage others. His courage, strong leadership, kindness, and dedication to protecting people are qualities that I admire.

My Favourite Quote

"Whenever you feel down, set your heart ablaze." Click to see the video

This is my favourite quote from Rengoku because it reminds me to keep going when I feel discouraged. To me, setting my heart ablaze means finding the motivation to continue working toward my goals even when things become difficult. This is something I can apply to my academic life because there are times when schoolwork can become challenging, but I should not allow those difficulties to stop me from trying.

My Personality Traits and Hobbies

Drawing

Drawing is one of my favourite hobbies because it allows me to be creative and express my ideas visually. It give me a way to relax while also improving my patience and attention to deatail. In my academic life, creativity helps me approach porblems from different perspectives.

Strong Leadership

I consider myself a strong leader because I am comfortable taking responsibility and helping other work towards a common goal. Leadership is important in my academic life because many assignments invole working with other students. Being able to communicate, organize task, and support my classmates helps me contribute effectively to group projects.

Listening to Music

Listening to music is something I enjoy in my free time. Music helps me relax and can also motivate me when I am studying or working on assignments. Taking short breaks to listen to music helps me manage stress and return to my schoolwork with a clearer mind.

LLMs as a Tool, Not a Crutch

To me, saying that LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot should be used as a tool and not a crutch means that students should use AI to help them learn instead of allowing it to do their work for them. As a Computer Science student, I can use LLMs to explain difficult topics, help me understand errors in my code, generate practice questions, and give me feedback on my work. However, I should still attempt my assignments myself and make sure I understand the answers. Relying on AI too much can prevent me from developing my own problem-solving skills. Therefore, I believe AI is most useful when I am stuck or need another explanation, but I should remain responsible for my own learning.
